## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-performance engineering through an architectural, tactile, and precision-driven aesthetic. It translates the kinetic principles of state-of-the-art ergonomic furniture—structural stability, adaptive articulation, and breathable luxury—into a digital environment.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** High-output professionals, industrial designers, executive buyers, and remote specialists investing in long-term musculoskeletal wellness and workspace refinement.
- **Impression:** Engineered precision over decorative excess. The UI evokes the sensation of brushed aeronautical alloys, tensioned performance mesh, and silent pneumatic calibration.

### Design Paradigm: Precision Architectural Minimalism
The system merges technical clarity with frosted glass accents. Key tenets include:
- Structural framing using dark slate chassis layers and hairline divisions.
- Calibrated mint-green focal points that represent active adjustment, health diagnostics, and precision mechanics.
- Ample negative space engineered around technical schematics and material textures.

## Colors

The palette mimics a high-performance studio setting: deep structural tones anchor the canvas, while hyper-focused mint accents call attention to interactive focal points.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#10B981`):** Active ergonomic adjustments, primary conversion points, validation states, and kinetic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#059669`):** Interactive hover states, active segment fills, and deep-accent badges.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`):** Aerodynamic airflow indicators, cooling fabric data layers, and secondary technical diagnostics.
- **Neutral Dark Canvas (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Structural foundation, viewports, console framing, and primary typographic weight in inverted contexts.
- **Neutral Light & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast text on dark backgrounds, physical card surfaces in mixed-mode environments, and luminous specular highlights.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#334155` for dark UI, `#E2E8F0` for light UI):** Hairline precision borders providing dimensional boundary without visual congestion.

### Accessibility Standards
All interactive pairs maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against their supporting backdrops. Text overlaid on `#10B981` defaults to `#0F172A` to preserve WCAG 2.1 AA legibility.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes geometric discipline, rhythmic vertical alignment, and high-density readability. Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the primary engine across display, editorial, and body roles, supplemented by Space Grotesk exclusively for ergonomic measurement readouts, telemetry, and product SKU specifications.

### Hierarchy & Composition Rules
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered at weights 700 and 800 with tight negative tracking to emulate architectural block carving. Avoid loose letter spacing on headlines above 24px.
- **Body:** Standardized at 400 weight with balanced 1.5–1.6 line heights. Body paragraphs in card shells must never exceed 65 characters per line to preserve scan-speed.
- **Labels & Spec Readouts:** Compact, high-clarity tokens rendered at 600 weight with uppercase transformation where specified to denote engineering parameters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point rhythmic grid, operating inside a 12-column dynamic framework designed for expansive product staging, multi-angle viewers, and detailed ergonomic adjustment matrices.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Composition
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin: `1rem` (`16px`), Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Components reflow into single-column vertical stacks. Interactive 3D model controls collapse into bottom sheets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin: `2rem` (`32px`), Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`). Technical specifications align in side-by-side matrices.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width of `1440px`. Margin: `3rem` (`48px`), Gutter: `2rem` (`32px`). Product visualization viewports lock to an 8-column span, while kinetic specification modules anchor within a 4-column sticky sidebar.

### Section Spacing Rules
Hero and high-impact product reveals require generous negative space (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` via clamp logic). Internal component density remains high and compact, prioritizing precision and efficient spatial consumption.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a layered composite model: physical distance is represented by surface luminosity, blur intensity, and razor-sharp border strokes rather than opaque, heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Solid `#0F172A`. Deepest plane, grounding product imagery and structural scaffolds.
- **Level 1 (Structural Shells & Shelves):** Background `#1E293B` at 70% opacity with `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`). Encapsulated by a `1px` stroke of `#334155`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Consoles):** Background `#1E293B` at 90% opacity, paired with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`, and an interior top-highlight inset border: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Adjusters & Flyouts):** Frosted glass construction: `#0F172A` at 85% opacity, `24px` backdrop blur, with a directional glow shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`. Outlined with a continuous `1px` border of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects industrial high-grade casting: functional curvatures combined with stable edges.

### Curvature Guidelines
- **Containers, Cards, & Panels:** Standardized to `0.5rem` (`8px`) to create crisp containment resembling forged structural components.
- **Large Contextual Surfacing (`rounded-lg`):** Configured to `1rem` (`16px`) for feature displays, configurator panels, and modal enclosures.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro-tags, lumbar-support adjustment chips, and active posture alerts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#10B981` background, `#0F172A` text, semi-bold weight. Border radius `8px`. Hover elevates to `#059669` accompanied by a `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)` glow.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with `1px` border in `#334155`, text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover transitions border to `#10B981` and text to `#10B981`, with background shifting to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.05)`.
- **Pneumatic Adjuster (Icon Action):** Square `44x44px` framed in `#1E293B`, `1px` border of `#334155`. Icons rendered in `#F8FAFC`. Active press yields a `0.97` scale compress mimicking physical switchgear.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specification Chips:** Dark slate background `#1E293B`, `1px` outline of `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`, uppercase label typography.
- **Active State / Calibration Badges:** Border radius `9999px`. Subtle mint background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#10B981`, prepended by a pulse indicator dot.

### Inputs & Sliders (Ergonomic Tuning)
- **Text Inputs:** `#0F172A` surface with a `1px` stroke of `#334155`. Focus immediately swaps stroke to `#10B981` with an ambient `2px` ring blur of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Ergonomic Range Sliders (Lumbar, Tilt, Tension):** Track width `4px`, color `#334155`. Filled track in `#10B981`. Thumb is a `16px` polished circle in `#FFFFFF` with a `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` drop shadow.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** `18x18px`, `4px` radius. Border `#334155` on `#1E293B`. Checked state transitions fill to `#10B981` with a dark checkmark graphic.
- **Segmented Configurator Tabs:** Enclosed in a `#0F172A` container with a `4px` internal pad. Active selection uses a elevated `#1E293B` panel with a `1px` border in `#334155` and mint label highlights.

### Product & Material Cards
- Standard layout combines a dark `#1E293B` semi-opaque surface, `1px` exterior `#334155` border, and high-resolution material viewports.
- Hover action triggers a smooth transition of the border to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4)` and a `2px` upward vertical translation.
